Tips for a clean scan

  • Light the page from two sides if you can; one lamp leaves a gradient that Shadow & gradient removal then has to undo.
  • A dark, matte desk mat makes edge detection almost perfect.
  • For text-only pages, Black & white gives the smallest files and the best OCR.
  • Auto-capture waits for stillness — keep your hand in frame until you want the shot.
